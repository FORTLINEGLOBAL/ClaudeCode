# Target Customer Scenario — Template

One page per scenario. One named person, one situation. Never a market category.

Produce 8–15 of these before scoring. Stop adding when new scenarios are only minor variations
on ones you already have.

---

## Scenario #__ : [short memorable name, e.g. "Priya the carve-out controller"]

**Segment sketch:** [industry, company type/size, geography — one line]

### The character
- **Name:** [invented first name]
- **Title / role:** [the actual job title]
- **Who they report to:** [and who holds the budget, if different]
- **What they are measured on:** [the metric that decides their year]

### A day in the life — BEFORE
[3–6 sentences. A specific moment, not a summary. What triggers the pain, what they do about it
today, what workaround they have built, who else gets pulled in, and how it feels. Concrete
detail is the point — this is what makes the scenario usable by intuition.]

**The moment it bites:** [one sentence — the specific event]

### Economic consequence
- **What the broken process costs:** [money / risk / time, quantified or bounded]
- **Who feels that cost:** [the economic buyer, by title]
- **What happens if they do nothing for another 12 months:** [the honest answer — if the answer
  is "not much," this scenario has a weak compelling reason to buy and will not survive Stage 1]

### A day in the life — AFTER
[3–6 sentences. Same moment, whole product in place. Describe the outcome the way the buyer
would describe it to a peer, because that is literally what has to happen for the segment to
develop.]

**The payoff, in their words:** [one sentence]

### Whole product notes
What, beyond what we ship, must exist for this "after" to be real:
- [ ] [integration / data / migration]
- [ ] [services, training, change management]
- [ ] [compliance, standards, procurement vehicle]
- [ ] [partner-supplied component]

### References and adjacency
- **Who else has this exact problem, and do they talk to each other?** [the referencing test]
- **Where do they talk?** [association, conference, forum, peer group, analyst]
- **If we win here, which adjacent niche opens next?** [bowling pin]

### Known competition here
- **Market alternative (incumbent taking this budget today):**
- **Product alternative (other disruptor with similar technology):**
- **Anyone already crossed into this niche?** [if yes, this is likely a fortified hill]
