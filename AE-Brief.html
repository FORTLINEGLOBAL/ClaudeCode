1 — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Organization Type
State Defense Conglomerates
State-owned defense industrial groups managing programs, facilities, and command infrastructure at national scale. E.g. Polska Grupa Zbrojeniowa (PGZ).
Organization Type
Government Ministries & Procurement Agencies
Defense ministries, armament agencies, and national security bodies responsible for procurement, infrastructure protection, and civil defense frameworks.
Organization Type
Critical Infrastructure Operators
Energy grids, national telecom providers, water utilities, data centers, and transport hubs — any operator whose facilities cannot afford downtime under threat.
Decision-Maker Profile
C-Suite & Board Level
CEO, VP Board, COO — strategic sign-off. These contacts approve budgets and can mandate facility protection programs across subsidiaries.
Decision-Maker Profile
Program Directors & Heads of Procurement
Program office directors, heads of purchasing, facility/security managers — the people who evaluate vendors and run the process. Essential second track.
Decision-Maker Profile
Military & Government Officials
Senior military officers (Brigadier General+), civil defense heads, and ministry officials responsible for national resilience strategy and civilian protection law.
Buying Trigger #1 — NATO Rearmament
Countries hitting 2–5% GDP defense spend are building active defense fast. But active defense without passive structural protection leaves every facility exposed when interception fails or is overwhelmed. The gap is the opening.
Buying Trigger #2 — Proximity to Active Threat
Eastern flank nations (Poland, Baltics, Finland, Romania) face credible, demonstrated missile and drone threats. Decision-makers in these countries have personal urgency. Use it. Reference the Ukraine conflict and NATO's eastern exposure directly.

Outreach Principles That Work For Fortline
Lead with IDF credentials, not the product. "IDF Home Front Command certified. 35 years under live threat conditions." opens every door faster than any feature description.
Mirror their threat reality. "Poland is building for a threat environment that looks a lot like the one we have been hardening against." — make the connection explicit, don't leave it implied.
Always CTA = "confidential protection assessment." Not a demo, not a deck, not a call. A classified survey under NDA. It sounds like something they already need to do — and they're right.
Use Israeli Trade Commission as a warm channel. TradeIL offices in Warsaw, Bucharest, Brussels, Stockholm. These officers can make introductions, validate Fortline's credibility, and open doors that cold email cannot. Always worth an intro call first.
Run parallel tracks. C-suite (CEO/VP Board) + Program Director + Head of Procurement — hit all three. One of them will respond, and internal pressure from multiple angles accelerates everything.
Keep cold emails under 120 words. Every winning email above is 5 sentences or fewer. The shorter the email, the more senior the reader assumes the sender to be. Long emails lose the room before the second paragraph.
