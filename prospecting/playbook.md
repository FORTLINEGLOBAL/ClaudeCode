# Fortline Global — Prospecting Playbook

Reference for the prospecting agent. Keep messaging grounded here and in
`../Europe One-Pager.html`. Treat country "angles" below as research *leads* to
verify with current sources, not as settled facts to assert in outreach.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

An organization is a strong fit when it owns or operates buildings/infrastructure
that are **physically exposed** to missile/drone/blast threats, sits **near a
front line or strategic target**, and has **budget authority or influence** over
resilience, security, or capital projects.

**Buyer & influencer roles to look for:**
- Government / civil defense: civil protection / emergency management director,
  ministry of interior / defense resilience leads, national security council
  staff, building-code / standards authorities.
- Critical infrastructure: CISO / physical security director, head of resilience
  / business continuity, facilities & engineering director, CTO/COO of the
  operator, capital-projects / procurement lead.
- Public buildings: hospital estates/facilities director, regional government
  building authority, emergency-services infrastructure lead.
- Defense primes / EPCs: business development, partnerships/alliances, head of
  fortification / protective-design engineering.

## Segments

1. **Government / civil defense** — defense & interior ministries, civil-defense
   agencies, NATO-aligned national bodies.
2. **Critical infrastructure** — power/energy, water, telecom, data centers,
   ports, rail/transport operators.
3. **Public buildings** — hospitals, schools, government buildings, emergency
   services.
4. **Defense primes / EPCs** — defense contractors and large engineering &
   construction firms that could integrate or resell Fortline.

## Country angles (verify before asserting)

### Poland
- Highest defense spender in NATO as a share of GDP; rapid rearmament.
- Long borders with Kaliningrad, Belarus, and Ukraine; front-line logistics hub
  (Rzeszów/Jasionka). Energy and military infrastructure in the east especially
  exposed. Repeated airspace-incursion concerns.

### Estonia
- NATO eastern flank, direct border with Russia; high defense spending as % GDP.
- "e-Estonia" — significant digital/data-center and government-IT footprint that
  needs physical protection. Narva region exposure.

### Romania
- Black Sea front; major NATO presence (e.g., Mihail Kogălniceanu base expansion).
- Documented drone-debris incidents on Romanian territory near the Danube/Ukraine
  border; Black Sea energy infrastructure.

### Latvia
- Baltic front-line state bordering Russia and Belarus; NATO enhanced forward
  presence; ongoing defense build-up and Baltic defense-line initiatives.

## Messaging pillars

1. **The passive-defense gap.** Active defense intercepts in the air; passive
   defense protects the building when interception fails or is saturated.
2. **Proven, not theoretical.** 35+ years of live operational experience in
   Israel.
3. **Practical to deploy.** Patented super-polymer reinforces walls from within —
   new build or retrofit, no demolition, no permits, deployed in days.
4. **Full stack.** Assessment & advisory (NDA Vulnerability Report) → physical
   hardening → population preparedness (GEM) → national regulatory framework
   (Israeli Standard 4422/4577).

## Calls to action (pick one per email)

- A confidential 20-minute introductory call.
- A no-obligation **Protection Assessment** briefing under NDA.
- An on-site engineering survey / vulnerability mapping for a priority site.

## Disqualifiers / cautions

- Avoid anything that reads as exploitative fear-mongering.
- Don't target individuals' personal data; B2B/B2G public business contacts only.
- If a target is in active crisis, lead with help/assessment framing, not sales.
