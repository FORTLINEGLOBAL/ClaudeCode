# Prospect Briefing — GAZ-SYSTEM S.A. (Operator Gazociągów Przesyłowych)

- **Country:** Poland
- **Segment:** Critical infrastructure (gas transmission, LNG terminal)
- **Date researched:** 2026-05-31
- **Fit score (1–5):** 5 — State gas TSO operating the Świnoujście LNG terminal, which the government has just run a major military/police anti-drone security drill on, citing Russian drone and espionage threats.

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
GAZ-SYSTEM is Poland's gas transmission system operator and operates (via subsidiary) the President Lech Kaczyński LNG Terminal in Świnoujście on the Baltic ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Awinouj%C5%9Bcie_LNG_terminal)). In Oct 2025 a large-scale security exercise — police, Border Guard and military — was run at the terminal, attended by the Interior Minister, explicitly citing drone attacks and Russian intelligence operations ([portseurope](https://www.portseurope.com/poland-stages-major-lng-terminal-security-drill/), [poland.news-pravda](https://poland.news-pravda.com/en/poland/2025/10/07/39605.html)). The terminal is to remain under "special protection" indefinitely. GAZ-SYSTEM also runs five Yamal-Europe compressor stations and Baltic Pipe compressor stations ([ABB](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/79148/abb-technology-enables-safe-and-smart-operations-at-the-polish-section-of-the-yamal-europe-gas-pipeline)).

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: Świnoujście LNG terminal (incl. second jetty); Yamal-Europe and Baltic Pipe compressor stations; >2,000 km of new transmission pipeline incl. eastern Poland.
- Size / scale: 100%-state-owned national gas TSO; operator of strategic LNG import gateway.
- Relevant recent news: Oct 2025 multi-agency security drill at Świnoujście ([portseurope](https://www.portseurope.com/poland-stages-major-lng-terminal-security-drill/)); Dec 2025 marked 10 years of the terminal and new certified services ([Gaz-System](https://www.gaz-system.pl/en/for-media/press-releases/2025/december/11-12-2025-strategic-project-for-real-independence-10-years-of-the-lng-terminal-in-swinoujscie.html)).
