# Prospect Briefing — PERN S.A. (Przedsiębiorstwo Eksploatacji Rurociągów Naftowych "Przyjaźń")

- **Country:** Poland
- **Segment:** Critical infrastructure (oil pipelines, fuel storage)
- **Date researched:** 2026-05-31
- **Fit score (1–5):** 5 — State oil-logistics monopoly with assets literally on the Belarus border; leadership is publicly framing fuel-logistics security as a "here and now" challenge.

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
PERN operates the Adamowo Tank Base, located directly on the border with Belarus, connected via the eastern Druzhba pipeline to the Płock oil base (annual capacity ~50 million tonnes) ([gem.wiki](https://www.gem.wiki/Brody-Adamowo_Oil_Pipeline), [PERN](https://www.pern.pl/en/services/crude-oil/crude-oil-transport/)). As Poland's sole crude-and-fuel pipeline/storage operator it is a top-tier strategic target. President Daniel Świętochowski publicly stated at ECIF 2025 (Dec 2025) that "security of fuel logistics is a here-and-now challenge" — an explicit, recent buying-intent signal ([PERN](https://www.pern.pl/en/2025/12/12/prezes-pern-na-ecif-2025-bezpieczenstwo-logistyki-paliwowej-to-wyzwanie-na-tu-i-teraz/)).

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: Adamowo Tank Base (on Belarus border), eastern Druzhba pipeline, Płock/Miszewko Strzałkowskie storage base, nationwide fuel terminals and pumping stations.
- Size / scale: State-owned national strategic operator; sole maritime-fed and pipeline crude/fuel logistics backbone for Poland.
- Relevant recent news: Dec 2025 — President Świętochowski headlines ECIF 2025 on fuel-logistics security ([PERN](https://www.pern.pl/en/2025/12/12/prezes-pern-na-ecif-2025-bezpieczenstwo-logistyki-paliwowej-to-wyzwanie-na-tu-i-teraz/)); ongoing Naftoport handling (PERN is connected to maritime oil intake).
