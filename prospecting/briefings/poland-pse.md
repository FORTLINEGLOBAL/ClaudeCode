## Why them (trigger / exposure)
PSE is the sole operator of Poland's high-voltage transmission grid (>15,000 km of >220 kV lines, 109 substations), 100% State Treasury-owned ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polskie_Sieci_Elektroenergetyczne)). It operates exposed southeastern nodes such as the 400 kV Rzeszów-Połaniec line connecting generation and load in the southeast near the front-line logistics region. Its 2040 plan commissions 28 new substations and ~7,000 km of new lines plus refurbishment of ~100 substations — a large hardening-relevant capital program ([PSE eng](https://www.pse.pl/web/pse-eng)). Substations are high-value, hard-to-replace, drone-vulnerable nodes.

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: 109 high-voltage substations (incl. southeastern Rzeszów-area nodes), control centers, transmission corridors.
