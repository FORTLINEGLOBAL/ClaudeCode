# Prospect Briefing — PGE Dystrybucja S.A.

- **Country:** Poland

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
PGE Dystrybucja is the largest Distribution System Operator in Poland, serving the central and eastern part of the country — the area closest to the Belarus and Ukraine borders, including the Lublin region ([interreg-baltic PDF](https://interreg-baltic.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/GoA-2.4_attachment_Energy-system-of-Poland.pdf)). It is headquartered in Lublin (Garbarska 21A) and operates the distribution grid feeding eastern Poland's population, military logistics corridors and border infrastructure. Distribution substations and control buildings across this belt are numerous, dispersed and physically unprotected — a realistic drone/blast and sabotage exposure on the eastern flank.

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: distribution substations, switching stations, regional control/dispatch buildings across central-eastern and eastern Poland.
- Size / scale: Largest Polish DSO by area served; part of state-controlled PGE Group.
- Relevant recent news: Board reconstituted April 2024 with President Jacek Drozd (a Lublin professor), continuing into 2025 ([Dziennik Wschodni](https://www.dziennikwschodni.pl/lublin/jest-nowy-zarzad-pge-dystrybucja-prezesem-zostal-profesor-z-lublina,n,1000339325.html)).
