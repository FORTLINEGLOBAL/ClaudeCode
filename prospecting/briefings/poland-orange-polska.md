## Why them (trigger / exposure)
Orange Polska is Poland's largest integrated telecom operator, with nationwide mobile (incl. new 700 MHz 5G with 99%-household coverage obligations to 2040) and its own Warsaw Data Hub (four 400 sqm data halls hosting network core and customer hosting) ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/orange-opens-new-data-center-in-warsaw-poland/), [globenewswire telecom report](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/17/3100897/28124/en/poland-telecom-operators-intelligence-report-2025-featuring-uke-orange-t-mobile-play-p4-polkomtel-plus-and-cyfrowy-polsat.html)). Telecom is foundational critical infrastructure: core sites, switching centers, the data hub and key eastern-region base stations underpin civil and military communications. Their physical envelopes (switching buildings, data halls, key towers) are generally not blast/drone-rated.
