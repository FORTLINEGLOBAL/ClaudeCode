## Why them (trigger / exposure)
The Port of Gdańsk hosts Naftoport, Poland's only maritime crude-oil intake point since Russian imports halted — a key link in the national oil logistics and refinery system ([ceenergynews](https://ceenergynews.com/oil-gas/gdansk-naftoport-expansion/), [Port Gdańsk](https://www.portgdansk.pl/en/events/a-new-chapter-in-the-history-of-the-northern-port-expansion-of-naftoport-in-the-port-of-gdansk-begins/)). A sixth liquid-fuel transshipment berth (for >300 m tankers) is under construction with DORACO, operational H2 2028. Poland is leading the NATO "Baltic Sentry" initiative to protect undersea energy links and strategic terminals; the port and its energy terminals are explicitly flagged as critical to national energy security ([Port Gdańsk 2025 results](https://www.portgdansk.pl/en/events/port-of-gdansk-maintains-its-strong-position-in-the-first-half-of-2025/)).

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: port quays, Naftoport liquid-fuel terminal, Port Północny (Northern Port), warehousing, control/admin buildings, rail/road links.
- Size / scale: Poland's largest seaport; record 2025 throughput.
- Relevant recent news: Naftoport sixth-berth expansion underway (operational 2028) ([ceenergynews](https://ceenergynews.com/oil-gas/gdansk-naftoport-expansion/)); strong 2025 figures ([polandatsea](https://www.polandatsea.com/port-of-gdansk-delivers-impressive-2025-performance/)); Baltic Sentry protection of Baltic energy links.
