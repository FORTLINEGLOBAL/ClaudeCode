# Prospect Briefing — Port Lotniczy "Rzeszów-Jasionka" Sp. z o.o.

- **Country:** Poland
- **Segment:** Critical infrastructure (transport / aviation logistics hub)
- **Date researched:** 2026-05-31
- **Fit score (1–5):** 5 — The single most strategically exposed civilian node in Poland: ~80–95% of Western military aid to Ukraine transits here, ringed by Patriot/NASAMS, ~90 km from the Ukrainian border.

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
Rzeszów-Jasionka is NATO's primary logistics artery for Ukraine; per Defense Express, ~80% of Western military aid (and aid from 40+ countries, ~95%) flows through it ([Mezha](https://mezha.net/eng/bukvy/poland-s-rzeszow-jasionka-hub-strengthens-ukraine-defense-and-nato-security/), [rzeszow-news](https://rzeszow-news.pl/jak-jasionka-stala-sie-kluczowym-hubem-wsparcia-dla-ukrainy/)). It is defended by US/German Patriot and Norwegian NASAMS III batteries — protection "unprecedented at other Polish airports" — with a dedicated NATO air-defense mission running 1 Dec 2025 to 1 Jun 2026 ([rp.pl](https://www.rp.pl/wojsko/art41718081-niemiecki-system-patriot-przez-pol-roku-bedzie-chronil-lotnisko-w-rzeszowie), [nato.news-pravda](https://nato.news-pravda.com/world/2025/08/09/57810.html)). The very presence of layered active defense is the argument: it exists because the threat is credible, and passive defense covers what gets through.

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: terminal buildings, cargo/handling facilities, fuel and warehousing infrastructure, control/ops buildings at the hub ~90 km from Ukraine.
- Size / scale: Regional airport operating company (Sp. z o.o.); now of national/NATO strategic importance.
- Relevant recent news: Aug 2025 — described as NATO's "frontline hub" ([nato.news-pravda](https://nato.news-pravda.com/world/2025/08/09/57810.html)); the NATO protection mission window 12/2025–06/2026; German forces concluding their Podkarpacie mission with open questions on who protects Jasionka next ([rzeszow-news](https://rzeszow-news.pl/niemieckie-wojska-koncza-misje-na-podkarpaciu-kto-bedzie-chronil-lotnisko-w-jasionce/)).
