# Prospect Briefing — ORLEN S.A. (Polski Koncern Naftowy ORLEN)

- **Country:** Poland
- **Segment:** Critical infrastructure (refining, petrochemicals, fuel)
- **Date researched:** 2026-05-31
- **Fit score (1–5):** 4 — Poland's integrated oil/petrochemical champion; Płock (16.3 Mt/yr) and Gdańsk (10.5 Mt/yr) refineries; has a dedicated board member for Security & Risk. Refineries are central/coastal, not on the eastern border.

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
ORLEN operates the Płock refinery (16.3 Mt/yr, one of CEE's most advanced) and the Gdańsk refinery (~10.5 Mt/yr, the Baltic anchor of its network) ([nsenergybusiness](https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/projects/plock-refinery-upgrade/), [ceenergynews](https://ceenergynews.com/oil-gas/orlen-group-to-make-new-investments-in-gdansk-refinery/)). These are sprawling, high-value, fire/blast-sensitive complexes central to national fuel security — and Poland's Supreme Audit Office has flagged fuel-safety risks around the refining system ([NIK](https://www.nik.gov.pl/en/news/poland-s-fuel-safety-in-the-shadow-of-the-merger-of-oil-giants.html)). ORLEN has a board-level Member for Security & Risk and is actively recruiting for that role (applications to 18 Feb 2026), signaling security as a live priority.

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: Płock and Gdańsk refineries, petrochemical units, control rooms, fuel terminals and depots nationwide.
- Size / scale: Largest Polish company; state-controlled integrated energy group.
- Relevant recent news: CEO Ireneusz Fáfara (since Apr 2024); board Member for Security & Risk role being re-recruited (to Feb 2026) ([Orlen](https://www.orlen.pl/pl/o-firmie/o-spolce/organy-i-struktura-spolki/zarzad-spolki)).
