# Prospect Briefing — PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe S.A. (PKP PLK)

- **Country:** Poland
- **Segment:** Critical infrastructure (rail infrastructure manager)
- **Date researched:** 2026-05-31
- **Fit score (1–5):** 4 — National rail infrastructure manager; owns the Rzeszów–Medyka and Małaszewicze/Medyka border corridors carrying military mobility and Ukraine logistics; EU-funded works underway.

## Why them (trigger / exposure)
PKP PLK is Poland's rail infrastructure manager. It received EUR 47.3m from the EC for the Rzeszów–Medyka section of the E30 line bordering Ukraine, with works that must also meet military-mobility needs ([railwaypro](https://www.railwaypro.com/wp/poland-ukraine-rail-border-development-agreement-signed/), [EIM](https://eimrail.org/2022/01/17/pkp-plk-railways-more-accessible-throughout-poland-thanks-to-billions-zlotys-of-investments-co-financed-by-european-union-funds/)). Nine Polish railway stations sit on the Ukraine border (Przemyśl, Medyka, Hrubieszów, etc.), and PLK's 2021–2027 plans explicitly target infrastructure for military traffic. Bridges, stations, signaling/control buildings and traction substations along these front-line corridors are physically exposed and hard to replace.

## Organization snapshot
- What they own/operate that is physically exposed: rail bridges, border stations, signaling and traffic-control buildings, traction substations along eastern corridors (Rzeszów–Medyka, Małaszewicze).
- Size / scale: National infrastructure manager; multi-billion-zloty EU-cofinanced investment program.
- Relevant recent news: Full board seated under President Piotr Wyborski (appointed 2024); in Mar 2025 Wyborski elected Vice-Chair of the European rail infrastructure managers' association (EIM) ([rynek-kolejowy](https://www.rynek-kolejowy.pl/wiadomosci/prezes-pkp-plk-w-zarzadzie-eim-122377.html), [sektorkolejowy](https://www.sektorkolejowy.pl/piotr-wyborski-zostal-powolany-na-stanowisko-prezesa-pkp-plk/)).
